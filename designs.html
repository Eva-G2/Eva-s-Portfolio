Graphic & Asset Designs

At first glance, graphic design and 3D modelling appear to be vastly different disciplines. Yet, when exploring complex multimedia forms, they reveal themselves as the inseparable, fundamental building blocks of any advanced visual system. The following collection is an archive of visual designs that are not meant to stand in isolation. Instead, I view these components as the cells of a larger organism, or the individual citizens of a broader society—each serving as a vital, foundational element designed to integrate into a much grander creative vision.
